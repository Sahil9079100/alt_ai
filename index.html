Why Image Alt Text Matters

Search engines cannot see product images the way shoppers do. Stores with optimized alt text can gain more visibility in Google Image Search and related shopping results.

  • Google uses alt text to index and rank product images
  • Helps visually impaired shoppers navigate your store
  • Required for ADA compliance - avoid legal risk
  • Improves overall page relevance signals for SEO
  • Increases visibility in Google Shopping results
