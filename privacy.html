Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the PixelBoost Shopify application operated by PixelBoost.

The Shopify App Store listing also links to the official PixelBoost privacy resource: Privacy policy.

1. Introduction

PixelBoost helps Shopify merchants improve image quality, accessibility, and store performance. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when merchants install or use PixelBoost through Shopify Admin.

2. Information We Collect

PixelBoost may access the following information to provide the app's features:

2.1 Store Owner Data

  • Store owner name, email address, phone number, and physical address
  • Shopify store name, domain, and relevant account identifiers

2.2 Product, Collection, and File Data

  • Products and collections needed for product image optimization
  • Product titles, descriptions, image URLs, image metadata, and current alt text values
  • Shopify admin files needed for compression, WebP conversion, and optimization reporting

2.3 Usage and Performance Data

  • Optimization status, bulk action history, app settings, and feature interactions
  • Page speed, Core Web Vitals, performance score, audit, and reporting data
  • Error logs and diagnostic information used to keep the app working reliably

3. How We Use Information

PixelBoost uses collected information to:

  • Generate AI alt text for product images
  • Compress product images and convert supported images to WebP
  • Analyze page speed impact and Core Web Vitals for product pages
  • Provide SEO score, audits, reporting, and performance dashboards
  • Respond to support requests and service-related communications
  • Improve app quality, reliability, security, and merchant experience

4. Third-Party Services

PixelBoost operates on Shopify and may use service providers for hosting, diagnostics, image processing, AI generation, analytics, and support. These providers are used only as needed to operate the app and deliver its features.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

PixelBoost does not sell merchant personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers, Shopify, legal authorities when required, or as needed to protect the rights, security, and operation of PixelBoost and its users.

6. Data Retention

PixelBoost retains data only as long as reasonably needed to provide the app, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, or support merchant requests. Alt text and image changes already applied to a Shopify store may remain in that store after uninstall unless removed by the merchant.

7. Data Security

PixelBoost uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Merchant Rights

Merchants may request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal data where applicable law provides those rights. To make a request, contact pixboost6233@outlook.com.

9. Cookies

PixelBoost may use essential technologies needed for app authentication, operation, security, and support. Shopify may also use cookies and similar technologies according to Shopify's own policies.

10. Children's Privacy

PixelBoost is intended for Shopify merchants and their authorized users. It is not directed to children, and PixelBoost does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

PixelBoost may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page or in an official policy resource, and continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies.
