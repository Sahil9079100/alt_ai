Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes! You get a 3-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required to start. You are only billed after the trial ends.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your Shopify admin. There are no cancellation fees or long-term contracts.

How does billing work?

You are billed $30 every 30 days through Shopify's billing system in USD. All billing is handled securely by Shopify.

What happens to my alt text if I cancel?

All alt text that has been applied to your images will remain in place. Cancelling only stops future optimization of new images.

Does it work with all Shopify plans?

Yes, ALT AI Image Optimizer works with all Shopify plans: Basic, Shopify, Advanced, and Shopify Plus.

How many images can I optimize?

The Basic plan includes unlimited image optimization. There are no per-image limits or caps on your catalog size.
